Principal Investigator

Dr Filipe Nascimento

Wellcome Career Development Award Fellow · Department of Neuromuscular Diseases, UCL Queen Square Institute of Neurology

Filipe graduated in Biochemistry at the University of Porto and completed an MSc in Neuroscience at the University of Lisbon, where he studied neuromuscular transmission dysfunction in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). He then did a PhD in Neurobiology at the University of St Andrews in Prof Gareth Miles' lab, focusing on cholinergic modulation of spinal circuits controlling locomotion.

He moved to UCL to work with Prof Marco Beato on spinal recurrent microcircuits in motor diseases, and was then awarded a Sir Henry Wellcome Postdoctoral Fellowship, hosted by Prof Rob Brownstone at the UCL Queen Square Institute of Neurology, to investigate the role of spinal microcircuits in aberrant excitability in ALS.

He is now a Wellcome Trust Career Development Award Fellow at UCL, leading an independent research programme that integrates animal and human neurophysiology to study spinal circuit dysfunction and neuronal resilience in neuromuscular diseases, particularly ALS.

Ragul Selvamoorthy

Research Fellow

Ragul trained in sport science and strength and conditioning before a PhD in neuromuscular physiology at the University of Stirling, where he studied the neuromuscular effects of resistance training. As a research fellow with Dr Eduardo Martinez-Valdes at the University of Birmingham, he ran a clinical trial of resistance training for tendon rehabilitation, using high-density surface EMG and ultrasound to study motor unit behaviour. He joined the lab in October 2026 to investigate how spinal circuits and motor neuron firing change over the course of MND/ALS, using non-invasive human electrophysiology such as high-density surface EMG and nerve stimulation.
